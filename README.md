# IBM HR Analytics | Workforce Analysis & Employee Attrition

## 📌 Project Overview

The **IBM HR Analytics Project** is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing workforce demographics, employee attrition, and factors influencing employee retention.

This project uses **SQL** for data cleaning, data validation, and exploratory data analysis, while **Microsoft Power BI** is used to develop an interactive dashboard that transforms raw employee data into meaningful business insights.

The primary objective is to identify workforce trends, understand employee attrition patterns, and provide data-driven insights that can support strategic HR planning and employee retention initiatives.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition trends and overall retention performance.
- Examine workforce distribution across departments and job roles.
- Identify demographic and organizational factors associated with employee turnover.
- Investigate the relationship between overtime, job roles, and employee attrition.
- Evaluate employee environment satisfaction across departments and job levels.
- Develop meaningful insights to support HR decision-making and workforce management.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

### 01. Data Preparation
- Imported the HR employee dataset.
- Explored the dataset structure, columns, and data types.
- Identified relevant attributes for workforce analysis.

### 02. Data Cleaning
- Examined data quality and consistency.
- Checked for duplicate records and missing values.
- Applied SQL techniques to prepare the dataset for analysis.

### 03. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyzed employee demographics and workforce distribution.
- Investigated attrition patterns across departments and job roles.
- Examined overtime, compensation, and employee satisfaction.

### 04. Data Modeling & KPI Development
- Prepared the data model for Power BI reporting.
- Developed DAX measures to calculate key HR performance indicators.
- Created measures for employee headcount, attrition, retention, and workforce metrics.

### 05. Dashboard Development
- Designed a two-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Incorporated KPI cards, charts, slicers, and interactive visualizations.
- Enabled analysis across departments, gender, job roles, and other employee attributes.

### 06. Business Insights
- Interpreted analytical findings to understand employee attrition.
- Identified workforce segments with comparatively higher attrition.
- Evaluated employee satisfaction and retention-related factors.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Research & Development has the highest employee headcount among the three departments.
- The overall employee attrition rate is 16.21%.
- Employees aged 26–35 represent the largest age group in the workforce.
- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate among the displayed job titles.
- Overtime and job roles provide useful dimensions for investigating employee attrition patterns.
- Environment satisfaction levels vary across departments and job levels.

## 💼 Business Value

This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to convert raw HR data into actionable business insights.

The dashboard helps HR teams monitor workforce trends, understand attrition patterns, evaluate employee satisfaction, and identify areas that may require attention to improve employee retention.
